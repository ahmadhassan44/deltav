How to replace a spreadsheet workflow

DeltaV engineering · 24 Sep 2026

Map the work around the file. Pick one workflow to move first. Set one source of truth with owners and permissions. Migrate a clean slice, train on the real process, then expand.

01

Signs the spreadsheet is the system

  • Nobody is sure which version is current.
  • Formulas hold business rules one person understands.
  • Data is copied in from other tools by hand.
  • Mistakes surface weeks later.

02

Step 1: map the work

Watch it run. Log every input and its source, every rule hidden in a formula, every handoff, every exception.

03

Step 2: pick the first slice

One workflow, one owner, visible payoff: intake, approvals, quoting, scheduling or status reporting. Move history after the new process works.

04

Step 3: set the source of truth

QuestionAnswer
Who sees it?Roles based on the work.
Who changes it?Named owners and approvals.
Where does it live?One authoritative record. Link, don't copy.
What if it fails?A visible exception, never a silent drop.

05

Step 4: migrate and train

Import clean data with a rollback plan. Test with the people who do the work. Keep the business rules; drop the spreadsheet habits.
