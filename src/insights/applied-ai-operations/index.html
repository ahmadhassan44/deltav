Where applied AI pays off in operations

DeltaV engineering · 24 Sep 2026

AI pays off when it speeds up a repeated decision with a clear user, accessible data, a tolerable error rate and a workflow that acts on the output. Start from the decision, not the model.

01

Four uses that work

UseWhat it does
RetrieveFinds answers across documents, records and procedures.
ClassifyRoutes requests, records and images. People review the uncertain ones.
InspectDetects defects or events in images, video and sensor data.
PredictFlags anomalies, forecasts demand, schedules maintenance.

02

Readiness checklist

  • Data. Exists, accessible, owned.
  • Definition of good. Agreed before the build.
  • Human boundary. What AI decides, what people review.
  • Home. Output lands where the work happens.
  • Feedback. Corrections flow back into the system.

03

Measure the system, not the model

QuestionMeasure
Faster?Time from intake to decision.
Reliable?Error and rework rate.
Used?Adoption; accepted vs corrected outputs.
Safe?Access logs, review paths, fallbacks.

No data foundation yet? Build that first.
