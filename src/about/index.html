What is deltaV Build?

Engagement

How it works

  1. Scope: free 30-minute call. Problem, payoff, plan.
  2. Build: working demos as we go.
  3. Run: we host, monitor and maintain it. Your data stays yours.

You work directly with the engineers who scope, build and maintain the software. We agree the data sources, approval rules and access needed for the work. Cost and timeline depend on complexity and the business case.

deltaV hosts the software on its own AWS infrastructure, on a monthly retainer. You get full use of it and own your data. deltaV retains the code, docs and methodology. Our scope is software, compute and AI.

Not the other DeltaV

Is deltaV Build the same as Emerson DeltaV?

No. Emerson DeltaV is a process control system. deltaV Build is an independent software team at deltav.build, not affiliated with Emerson.

Why the name deltaV?

Δv (delta-v) is the astronautics measure of the impulse needed to change an object's trajectory. deltaV adds velocity to a business. Delta-v explained →
