Ownership and trust

Your code, your data

Who owns the code?

You. Source, deployment, documentation and access are handed over in full.

Do you sign NDAs?

Yes. Before the scoping call, if you want.

Can we see past work?

Most of our past work is under client NDA, so we don't publish it. On the scoping call we walk you through how we solved similar problems: approach, architecture, trade-offs.

It stays yours. We never reuse client data or use it to train models.

How do you handle security?

Access control, encryption and audit trails are in scope from day one. Bring your compliance requirements to the scoping call.
