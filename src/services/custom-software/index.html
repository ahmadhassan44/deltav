Service 01 / Custom software

Custom software. Built around your work.

Custom software is an application built around a business process that off-the-shelf tools don't fit. deltaV scopes it, builds it, and hosts and maintains it. You get full use of the software, and your data stays yours.

What we build

Visibility

Dashboards

Jobs, inventory, customers and performance in one view.

External

Portals

Customers and partners submit, track and act without email chains.

Example

One job from request to review

A quoting workflow can bring the request, drawings, estimate and approval into one record. An accepted quote can then feed the job paperwork, with actual hours available for the next estimate.

The CNC quoting prototype shows that approach with sample data. For your operation, we would map the records, permissions and connection to your existing tools before building it.

Outcomes

What changes

  • Data entered once.
  • Business rules move out of spreadsheets and into the system.
  • Every job shows who has it and what's next.
  • Software fits the business, not the other way round.
