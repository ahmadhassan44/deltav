## Positioning

Keep the software you already use. We build the missing intelligence and workflows between it. Don't pitch "we build custom software". It's too generic.

## Verified facts in use

- ServiceTitan 2026 Commercial Specialty Contractor Industry Report: only 20% on a single platform; 34% use 2–3 systems, 25% use 4–6. The "80% use 2–10+ systems" wording is **not** in the source.
- Procore: QBO payment export only with Procore Pay; 500+ integrations (procore.com, 2026-09).
- Paperless Parts: 800+ shops, ERP list on /integrations/, pricing not public, API behind a demo request. The Capterra QuickBooks complaint says the fix is "in the works". Don't cite it as a current gap.
