What drives cost

DriverPushes cost up when
ScopeMany workflows go into the first release.
IntegrationsMany systems, no APIs, unreliable sources.
DataRecords are messy, duplicated or scattered.
RolesMany user types, permissions and approval paths.
ComplianceAudit trails, retention or regulated data.
AIEvaluation, human review and monitoring are required.

What drives timeline

Scope and decision speed. Most delay comes from waiting on access, data and answers, not from code. One owner with authority to decide keeps the build moving.

How to cut cost

  • Ship one workflow first.
  • Integrate before replacing.
  • Clean only the data the first release needs.
  • Keep commodity features in products you already pay for.
  • Demand weekly demos. Catch wrong turns in days, not months.

Fixed quote vs hourly

Hourly billing puts scope risk on you. A fixed quote forces scope to be defined up front. DeltaV scopes first, then quotes one price, with weekly demos until handover.
