ScopeMany workflows go into the first release.

DataRecords are messy, duplicated or scattered.

ComplianceAudit trails, retention or regulated data.
AIEvaluation, human review and monitoring are required.

What drives timeline

The workflow, system access and decision process all affect the timeline. Missing data, unclear approval rules or an unavailable integration need to be resolved as part of scoping. One person who can explain the work and coordinate decisions helps the team get clear answers.

How to cut cost

  • Ship one workflow first.
  • Integrate before replacing.
  • Clean only the data the first release needs.
  • Keep commodity features in products you already pay for.
  • Review working software against the process and its exceptions, so wrong assumptions are caught.

What to bring to the scoping call

  • A request or record you can share, and the steps it follows today.
  • The systems and spreadsheets involved, including where data is entered twice.
  • Who approves the work, and an example of an exception they handle.
  • The business case: time spent, mistakes to avoid or work that is delayed.
