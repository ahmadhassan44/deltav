What job shop quoting software can't know

  • Your machines. Which cell runs it, and how fast in practice.
  • Your actual hours. What similar parts really took on your floor.
  • Your customers. Who pays on time, who changes the drawing twice.
  • Your wins. Which prices won, which lost, and by how much.
  • Your inbox. RFQs that arrive as email with PDFs attached.

That is where shops differ, and where the margin is. It lives in your ERP, your spreadsheets and your estimator's head.

Questions to ask before you buy

  1. Does it learn from our actual hours, or only from geometry and rates?
  2. Does it read 2D drawings, or only 3D models?
  3. Does a won quote reach our ERP without retyping?
  4. Can we export our data if we leave?
