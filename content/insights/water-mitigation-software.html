What to look for in water mitigation software

  • Field first. Works on a phone, on site, for the tech.
  • Drying logs. Daily readings by room and material, moisture maps.
  • Photos by room. Labelled and tied to the line they prove.
  • Signatures. Work authorization signed on the first visit.
  • Xactimate connection. Scope goes to the estimate without retyping.
  • Accounting connection. Invoices and payments reach QuickBooks or your ledger.

Where water mitigation software stops

  • Your rules. Company procedures and carrier requirements that differ by job.
  • Missed rooms. Nothing forces a reading in the room next door.
  • Unsafe demolition. Hazard checks before a cut, based on the building.
  • Proof per line. Lines go out without the reading or photo behind them.
  • Retyping. Field tool, estimate, job tool and accounting don't all talk.
