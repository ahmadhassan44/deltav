What equipment rental software for small business covers

  • Inventory. What you own, where it is, when it's free.
  • Bookings. Quotes, reservations and contracts.
  • Billing. Invoices, deposits, payments, damage charges.
  • Yard. Check-out and return from a phone, with photos.
  • Maintenance. Service and repair history per unit.
  • Online. A storefront or portal where customers book.

For bare rentals, that is most of the job.

Crane tickets to QuickBooks, step by step

  1. Capture. The operator logs hours and gets the signature on a phone. Paper tickets get photographed.
  2. Check. Flag missing signatures, P.O. numbers and hours that don't add up.
  3. Split. Operator hours to jobs and pay codes.
  4. Apply. Each customer's minimums, travel and standby rules.
  5. Approve. Billing signs off on the Friday review.
  6. Send. Approved invoices to QuickBooks through its supported imports or connection. Hours to payroll.
