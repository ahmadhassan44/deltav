Where shops still retype

From reviews on Capterra, September 2026. The product rates well overall; these are the integration complaints:

  • "We do not currently have this software integrated with our ERP system so there are some redundant data entries."
  • "The only con is it will not currently integrate with our manufacturing system."

Other common gaps around any quoting tool:

  • RFQs arrive by email with drawings attached and get uploaded by hand.
  • Quoted hours are never compared to actual hours from the floor.
  • Won quotes get retyped into travelers and inspection sheets.

What a custom layer adds

GapWhat the layer does
Unsupported or customised ERPPushes won quotes into the ERP as jobs, by API, import or database.
AccountingWrites orders and invoices to your accounting system with the quote attached, where no native sync covers it.
RFQ intakeReads the inbox, files drawings and opens the quote with due dates set.
Quote vs actualJoins quoted hours with actual hours per job, so the next estimate starts closer.
Shop paperworkGenerates the traveler and inspection plan from the won quote.

The estimator still approves every price. The quoting tool and the ERP stay the systems of record.

How to start

  1. List every retype. Where does a person copy data from one screen to another?
  2. Check native first. Confirm what the integration covers for your ERP and version.
  3. Build the costliest gap. Usually job creation or quote-vs-actual.
