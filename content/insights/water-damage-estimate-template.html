Prices come from your estimating tool, not the template. The template decides what you prove.

What adjusters cut, and why

  • Equipment days with no log. No daily reading, no proof the machine was needed.
  • Demolition with no reading. Nothing shows the material was wet.
  • Rooms with no photo. The room is on the estimate, not on camera.
  • Equipment not sized to the room. Count doesn't match the room size.
  • Antimicrobial with no reason. Category missing from the file.
  • Drying past the goal. Readings hit dry, equipment kept running.
  • Late signature. Work started before the authorization was signed.

Every cut has the same cause: missing documentation. The work was done; the file doesn't show it.

How to stop the cuts

  1. Capture on the first visit. Rooms, readings and photos before the tech leaves.
  2. Check the room next door. Read the walls and floors around every wet room.
  3. Tie every line to proof. No reading or photo, no line.
  4. Log every day. Same rooms, same points, dated.
  5. Sign before work starts. On site, on the phone.
  6. Review before sending. Flag lines with no backup.

A paper or spreadsheet template depends on the tech remembering all six. A field app enforces them. If the template lives in Excel today, see replacing spreadsheets with custom software. For the build itself, see custom software.
