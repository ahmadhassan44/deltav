What a small machine shop ERP must do

JobWhat good looks like
QuotePrices from your rates; won quotes become jobs without retyping.
JobRouting, material and drawing revision on one record.
FloorStatus and hours recorded as work happens.
PurchasingMaterial and outside processing ordered against the job.
QualityInspection records tied to the part and revision.
InvoiceShipped jobs reach accounting once.

Everything else is optional for a small shop.

Machine shop ERP software, as vendors describe it

From vendor websites, October 2026. Their words, not a ranking:

  • ProShop. ERP, quality and shop-floor system in one, aimed at precision machine shops; says 700+ precision manufacturers run on it.
  • JobBOSS². Job and machine shop management for small and mid-sized manufacturers; cloud or on-premise.
  • Fulcrum. Cloud manufacturing software for precision CNC shops and sheet metal fabricators.

Most job shop management software covers the table above. The difference is fit to your floor and how much your team actually uses it.

Fill gaps without migrating

  1. List every retype. Where does someone copy data from one screen, sheet or email to another?
  2. Check the module. Many gaps are features you own but never set up.
  3. Check the access. API, import files or database. That decides what a layer can do.
  4. Build the costliest gap. Often RFQ intake, floor status or quote vs actual.
