The Procore NetSuite connector

From Procore's documentation, checked October 2026:

  • Who runs it. Procore partner Calance manages the connection. The marketplace listing is "NetSuite by Calance".
  • Requires Procore Project Financials including Invoicing, and NetSuite release 2022.
  • Approval. An accounting approver in Procore starts every export. Subcontractor invoices can skip that step if Direct Export is turned on.
  • Timing. Exports go on approval. Job costs come back once a day, or on request.
  • Scope. One NetSuite company, one subsidiary, per Procore site.

Sources: Procore's NetSuite overview and things to know. Availability depends on your country; ask Procore.

What the Procore NetSuite integration doesn't sync

Procore's things-to-know page lists these as not syncing:

  • Budgets.
  • Commitments (subcontracts, purchase orders) and their change orders.
  • Prime contracts and prime contract change orders.
  • Timecards and timesheets.

Also documented: single-tier cost codes, sub jobs and project-specific cost codes are not supported. At most ten cost types can be mapped to their own GL expense accounts.

Filling the gaps with a custom layer

  1. Pick one gap. The record retyped most often, usually commitments or invoices.
  2. Agree the owner. Which system is the source for each field.
  3. Move it. Through NetSuite's supported imports or API, with approval kept.
  4. Confirm. Check NetSuite accepted it and store the record reference.
  5. Leave the rest. Whatever the connector carries, it keeps carrying.
