What bid leveling in Procore does

From Procore's own documentation (checked October 2026):

  • Side by side. The Bidding tool's Bid Leveling tab compares bids line item by line item against your budget or a chosen benchmark.
  • Adjust. Admins can override a leveled amount, exclude a line from the totals and add a note explaining why.
  • Award. Leveled bids convert to a subcontract or purchase order.
  • Prerequisite. Bids can only be leveled after a bid form exists and bids have been received.

Sources: Procore's Level Bids for a Bid Form and Review Submitted Bids. Check what your subscription includes.

Where Procore bid leveling stops

Procore's own note: if a bidder replies to the invitation email instead of filling in the bid form, you review their submission in the attachments section (Procore, October 2026). That proposal is a PDF someone has to read.

  • Format. Many subs send their own proposal, not your form.
  • Fine print. Exclusions and qualifications live in their wording, not in a line item.
  • Scope match. Their line items rarely match yours one to one.

What AI bid leveling can do

  1. Read. Pull prices, line items and terms from PDF, spreadsheet or email proposals.
  2. Normalize. Map each bidder's lines onto your scope items.
  3. Flag exclusions. Mark scope excluded, not mentioned, or carried by only one bidder.
  4. Trace. Link every number to the page it came from.
  5. Draft questions. List what to ask each bidder before award.

A read the system isn't sure of goes to the estimator to confirm. Nothing is filled in silently.
