What AI estimating tools do

Most "AI estimating" tools are AI takeoff. They read drawings and return quantities:

  • Detect. Walls, rooms, doors, windows and fixtures on the plan set.
  • Measure. Lengths, areas and counts per sheet.
  • Search. Find every instance of a symbol or note.
  • Export. Quantities out to Excel, PDF or estimating software.

Togal's own features page says it: "Simply export your quantities out of Togal and into your own estimation software or excel spreadsheet" (Togal features, September 2026). The count is automated. The price is not.

What a custom workflow adds

  1. Map. Takeoff quantities land on your line items and cost codes automatically.
  2. Price. Each line pulls your current unit cost and crew rate, with the source shown.
  3. Compare. Flag lines that differ from similar past jobs.
  4. Level. Read sub quotes and line them up for scope gaps.
  5. Learn. Record the outcome of every bid against its numbers.

The estimator still sets the markup and signs off. The workflow removes the retyping and puts history on screen.
