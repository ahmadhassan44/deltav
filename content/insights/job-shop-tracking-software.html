Why paper travelers fall short

  • Status is only known by walking the floor.
  • Hours are written at the end of the shift, from memory.
  • Scrap and rework notes get lost or never reach the office.
  • The traveler lives with the parts, so the office can't see it.
  • Someone retypes it into the ERP, or no one does.

Track less, not more. Every field an operator skips makes the rest less trusted.

From paper to live status

  1. Keep the traveler. Print a barcode on it. Paper stays with the parts.
  2. Put a tablet at each cell. Scan the traveler, tap start, tap stop.
  3. Show the board. Every open job, its operation and anything on hold.
  4. Flag the late ones. Jobs behind their due date surface on their own.
  5. Feed it back. Actual hours sit next to quoted hours for the next quote.

Start with one cell. Fix what operators push back on, then roll out.
