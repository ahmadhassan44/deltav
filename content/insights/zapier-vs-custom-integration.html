Where Zapier wins

  • Few steps, low volume.
  • Standard apps with ready-made connectors.
  • A delay of a few minutes costs nothing.
  • One person owns and understands every Zap.

If that's you, stay. No-code is the fastest way to prove a workflow is worth automating.

Five signs you've outgrown it

SignWhat it looks like
CostThe bill climbs with every order or lead. Each successful action step is a billed task.
SpeedPolling triggers check every 1–15 minutes, depending on plan. Customers notice the lag.
LogicDozens of Zaps, paths and filters. Nobody can say what runs when.
FailuresA step errors, nobody sees it, and records go missing for a week.
DataThe work needs joins, deduplication or history that a step-by-step flow can't hold.

Two or more of these, and the workaround has become the system.

How to switch without breaking operations

  1. Inventory. Every Zap: trigger, steps, owner, monthly tasks.
  2. Rank. By cost, failure rate and business impact.
  3. Rebuild the top flow. Run it alongside the Zap and compare outputs.
  4. Cut over. Once outputs match, pause the Zap. Delete it a month later.
  5. Repeat, or stop. Many flows can stay on Zapier for good.
