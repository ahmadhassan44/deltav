Where hand checks usually remain

Integrations move records. The checks before a record is right to move are often still done by a person.

  • Tickets. A missing signature, P.O. number or end time holds the invoice.
  • Hours. One operator's day split across jobs, travel, overtime and pay rates.
  • Billing rules. Minimums, travel time and standby that differ by customer.
  • Third-party paper. Rerent tickets and customer forms that arrive as photos or PDFs.
  • Friday. Someone works out which jobs are ready to bill and which are stuck.

Ticket to cash, step by step

  1. Capture. Read each ticket, digital or photographed, and match it to the job.
  2. Check. Flag missing signatures, P.O. numbers and hours that don't add up.
  3. Split. Allocate operator hours to jobs and pay codes.
  4. Apply. Run each customer's billing rules and draft the invoice lines.
  5. Approve. Dispatch or billing signs off. Exceptions stay visible.
  6. Hand off. Clean lines go to NexGen, accounting and payroll.

NexGen stays the system of record. The layer only checks and moves data. The crane quote-to-cash demo shows tickets, hours and the Friday billing review; the pre-payroll review demo shows the hours check.
