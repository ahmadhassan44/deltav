Why quotes are slow

  • Every request waits for one person to read the drawing or spec.
  • Hours and prices come from memory or a private spreadsheet.
  • Options that can't be built together get quoted anyway.
  • Nobody compares quoted hours to actual hours.
  • Won jobs get retyped into travelers, orders and inspection sheets.

What the system does

  1. Intake. RFQs and inquiries land in one queue with due dates.
  2. Read. Drawings and specs parsed for material, tolerances, finishes and quantities.
  3. Estimate. Your cost rules and your past jobs, not a generic rate table.
  4. Review. The estimator sees every assumption and approves or edits.
  5. Send. A clean quote letter, margins hidden.
  6. Learn. Won quotes become the job; actual hours feed the next estimate.

How to start

  • Pull the last year of quotes, wins and actual hours.
  • Write down the rules the estimator applies without thinking.
  • Automate reading and lookup first; leave pricing judgment to the estimator.
